Key Phrases

Ordering Food

Je voudrais... = I would like...

J'ai faim = I'm hungry

J'ai soif = I'm thirsty

Bon appétit! = Enjoy your meal!

Verbs

  • boire = to drink
  • manger = to eat
  • prendre = to take / to have
  • choisir = to choose
  • commander = to order
  • payer = to pay

Practice Activities

📝 Exercise 1: Restaurant Menu Creation (20 mins)

Dual Coding + Retrieval:

  1. Design a French restaurant menu with at least 3 sections (hors d'œuvre, plat, dessert)
  2. Include 4-5 items in each section with French names only
  3. Add prices in euros (€)
  4. Swap menus with a friend or family member - can you order a 3-course meal in French?
  5. Example: "Je voudrais le pâté, puis le poulet rôti, et une tarte aux pommes pour le dessert."

🗣️ Exercise 2: At the Café Role Play (15 mins)

Active Recall + Speaking:

  1. Write a conversation between a waiter and customer at a French café
  2. Customer: Order a drink and a snack using "Je voudrais..."
  3. Waiter: Ask questions, suggest items, bring the bill
  4. Include at least 5 food/drink items from the vocabulary list
  5. Practice saying it aloud - can you do it without reading?

🃏 Exercise 3: Food Category Flashcards (20 mins)

Organization + Spaced Practice:

  1. Create flashcards sorted by category: drinks, meat/fish, vegetables, fruit, desserts
  2. Front: French word + gender. Back: English + use in a sentence
  3. Example front: "la pomme (f.)" Back: "apple - J'aime les pommes"
  4. Test yourself: shuffle all categories together, can you still remember them?
  5. Use the Leitner system (Box 1, 2, 3) to focus on tricky words

✍️ Exercise 4: Partitive Articles Challenge (10 mins)

Grammar Application: Complete these with du/de la/de l'/des:

  1. "Je voudrais ___ pain et ___ confiture" (I would like bread and jam)
  2. "Il mange ___ poulet avec ___ frites" (He eats chicken with chips)
  3. "Elle boit ___ eau minérale et ___ jus d'orange" (She drinks mineral water and orange juice)
  4. "Nous prenons ___ fromage après le plat principal" (We have cheese after the main course)
  5. Check your answers, then create 3 more sentences yourself!

🎨 Exercise 5: Food Pyramid (15 mins)

Dual Coding + Organization:

  1. Draw a food pyramid or healthy eating plate
  2. Label each section in French: fruits, légumes, viandes, produits laitiers, etc.
  3. Add at least 3 French food items in each section
  4. Write 3 sentences about healthy eating: "Il faut manger beaucoup de fruits..."

🔄 Exercise 6: Mixed Practice (15 mins)

Interleaving:

  1. Don't just practice food! Mix it with other topics you've learned
  2. Write sentences combining food with daily routine, opinions, and future plans
  3. Example: "Le matin je prends des céréales. J'adore le chocolat chaud mais je préfère le thé. Demain je vais manger au restaurant."
  4. This forces your brain to work harder and remember better!
