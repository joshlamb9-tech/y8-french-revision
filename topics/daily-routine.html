Example Sentences

Le matin je prends souvent une douche.

= I often take a shower in the morning.

Pour le petit déjeuner je prends des céréales et du chocolat chaud.

= For breakfast I have cereal and hot chocolate.

Je quitte la maison à huit heures et je rentre vers quatre heures et quart.

= I leave the house at 8 o'clock and I return home at quarter past 4.

Le soir je me brosse les dents, je range ma chambre et je me couche.

= In the evening I brush my teeth, tidy my room and go to bed.

📖 Reading Comprehension

Narrator: Chloé (age 13)

Je m'appelle Chloé et j'ai treize ans. En semaine, je me lève à six heures et demie parce que l'école commence à huit heures. Je me douche, puis je me brosse les dents et je m'habille. Au petit-déjeuner, je mange des céréales et je bois du jus d'orange. Je n'ai pas le temps de prendre un grand repas le matin ! Je vais à l'école à pied avec ma meilleure amie, Sophie — ça prend environ vingt minutes. Le soir, je fais mes devoirs après le dîner parce que j'aime regarder la télé d'abord. Je me couche normalement à neuf heures et demie, mais le week-end, je me couche beaucoup plus tard. Le week-end, je me lève à dix heures — c'est fantastique !

Questions

Answer in English.

  1. What time does Chloé get up on school days, and why?
  2. What does she eat and drink for breakfast?
  3. How does Chloé get to school, and how long does it take?
  4. When does Chloé do her homework, and what does she do before it?
  5. What is the difference between her bedtime on school nights and at the weekend?
Show answers
  1. Q1: She gets up at half past six because school starts at eight o'clock.
  2. Q2: She eats cereal and drinks orange juice.
  3. Q3: She walks with her best friend Sophie; it takes about twenty minutes.
  4. Q4: She does homework after dinner; she watches TV first.
  5. Q5: On school nights she goes to bed at half past nine; at the weekend she goes to bed much later.

Practice Activities

📝 Exercise 1: My Daily Routine Paragraph (15 mins)

Elaboration Practice:

  1. Write a paragraph describing your typical school day in French
  2. Include: wake up time, breakfast, leaving home, school activities, returning home, evening routine, bedtime
  3. Use at least 5 time expressions (le matin, vers huit heures, etc.)
  4. Use at least 3 reflexive verbs (je me lève, je me lave, je me couche)
  5. Read it aloud - does it sound natural?

🕐 Exercise 2: Time Telling Challenge (10 mins)

Active Recall:

  1. Draw 6 clock faces showing: 3:00, 7:15, 9:30, 11:45, 12:00, 4:20
  2. Write the time in French under each one
  3. Cover them and write from memory: "At what time do you wake up? eat breakfast? leave home?"
  4. Answer in complete sentences: "Je me réveille à..."

🃏 Exercise 3: Reflexive Verb Conjugation Cards (15 mins)

Retrieval Practice:

  1. Create flashcards for reflexive verbs: se lever, se laver, se coucher, se brosser les dents
  2. Front: infinitive + English. Back: full conjugation (je me lève, tu te lèves, il/elle se lève...)
  3. Test yourself: can you write all forms without looking?
  4. Mix them up with regular verbs you know - don't just practice one type!

✍️ Exercise 4: Translation Race (10 mins)

Speed Retrieval: Translate these as fast as you can, then check:

  1. "I wake up at 7 o'clock"
  2. "She takes a shower in the morning"
  3. "We usually have breakfast at half past seven"
  4. "I leave the house at quarter to nine"
  5. "They go to bed at 10 o'clock in the evening"

🎨 Exercise 5: Comic Strip Timeline (20 mins)

Dual Coding:

  1. Draw a 6-panel comic strip of your daily routine
  2. Each panel = one activity (waking up, breakfast, school, homework, dinner, bed)
  3. Add French captions with times: "À sept heures, je me réveille"
  4. Use thought bubbles for opinions: "J'adore le petit déjeuner!"
  5. Test: can someone else understand your day just from the pictures and French?

🔄 Exercise 6: Interleaving Practice (15 mins)

Mix It Up:

  1. Don't just practice daily routine! Mix in other topics you've learned
  2. Write 3 sentences about your routine, 2 about food, 2 about school
  3. Example: "Le matin je prends des céréales. J'aime le français mais je n'aime pas les maths. Je me couche à dix heures."
  4. This stops your brain getting too comfortable with one pattern!
