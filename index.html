Exam Tips
  • 🎯 Practice all four skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading & Writing
  • 📋 Learn vocab in context using sentence builders — not bare word lists
  • ⏰ Review grammar little and often. Cramming the night before doesn’t work.
  • 🔍 Use the checklist to find weak spots before the exam finds them for you
  • 🔖 Check your level: are you sitting Level 1 Foundation or Level 2 Higher?
  • 📝 Attempt past papers timed — treat them like the real thing
