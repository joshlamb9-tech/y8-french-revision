⏪ Perfect Tense (Passé Composé) Level 2

Talk about completed actions in the past

← Grammar Hub Checklist

⏱️ Pomodoro Timer

Set a timer for 25 minutes. Work through this page using the activities below. When the timer goes off, take a 5-minute break!

When to Use the Perfect Tense

  • Talk about things you did in the past: J'ai joué au foot (I played football)
  • Describe completed actions: Je suis allé au cinéma (I went to the cinema)
  • Say what happened: Il a fait ses devoirs (He did his homework)

How to Form the Perfect Tense

auxiliary verb (avoir or être) + past participle

With AVOIR (Most Verbs) Both Levels

Most verbs use avoir as the auxiliary verb:

J'ai joué = I played / I have played

Tu as regardé = You watched / You have watched

Il a mangé = He ate / He has eaten

Nous avons fini = We finished / We have finished

Vous avez vendu = You sold / You have sold

Ils ont choisi = They chose / They have chosen

Making Past Participles

Verb Type Rule Example
-ER verbs Remove -er, add -é jouer → joué
-IR verbs Remove -ir, add -i finir → fini
-RE verbs Remove -re, add -u vendre → vendu

⚠️ Agreement with ÊTRE

When you use être, the past participle must agree with the subject:

  • Je suis allé (boy) / Je suis allée (girl)
  • Nous sommes allés (boys or mixed) / Nous sommes allées (girls only)

Add -e for feminine, -s for plural, -es for feminine plural

Negatives in the Perfect Tense

Put ne...pas around the auxiliary verb (avoir or être):

Je n'ai pas joué = I didn't play

Je ne suis pas allé = I didn't go

Nous n'avons pas fait = We didn't do/make

📝 Revision Activities

Pick at least TWO of these evidence-based techniques:

1. Blurting (Retrieval Practice)

Time: 5 minutes

Close this page. On a blank sheet, "blurt" everything you remember about the perfect tense:

  • How do you form it?
  • Which verbs use avoir? Which use être?
  • Write out MRS VAN DER TRAMP from memory
  • List 5 irregular past participles

Check back with a different color pen. Circle your gaps!

2. Flashcard Boxing - Irregular Past Participles

Time: 15 minutes

  1. Make flashcards: Front = infinitive (faire), Back = past participle (fait)
  2. Three piles: "Don't Know", "Sort Of", "Nailed It"
  3. Test yourself - get it right? Move up. Wrong? Back to pile 1
  4. Focus on the "Don't Know" pile until it's empty

3. The 3-2-1 Technique

Time: 5 minutes

After studying this page, write:

  • 3 key facts about the perfect tense
  • 2 connections to other topics (e.g., "avoir in present tense", "agreement with être")
  • 1 question you still have

4. Sentence Builder Challenge

Time: 10 minutes

Write 10 sentences in the perfect tense from memory. Include:

  • 5 with avoir (include at least 2 irregular past participles)
  • 5 with être (MRS VAN DER TRAMP verbs)
  • Make sure some are negative!

Then check your grammar carefully.

5. Sketch It - MRS VAN DER TRAMP

Time: 5 minutes

Create a visual memory aid for MRS VAN DER TRAMP. Draw a simple picture or diagram that helps you remember which verbs use être. Stick figures are fine!

🧠 Interleaving Tip

Don't just practice perfect tense by itself! Mix it with present tense practice so you learn WHEN to use each one:

  • Write 5 sentences in present tense
  • Write 5 sentences in perfect tense
  • Translate mixed sentences from English to French

This mixing makes your brain work harder, which creates stronger memories!
